# GNOME UI

Desktop recording controls and live dictation presentation.
Dictation terms are defined in
[Active Listener](../active-listener/GLOSSARY.md).

## Language

**Panel indicator**:
The top-bar representation of service availability and recording state.

**Transcript overlay**:
The on-screen presentation of the current recording's transcript
and audio spectrum.

**Spectrum**:
The audio-level visualization shown during recording.

**Rewrite control**:
The recording-menu toggle for the available rewrite capability.
